## Certificates

The certificates page allows one to review all of the issued or imported certificates in the OTPKI system.  
One can filter by subject fields and sort by a number of properties.  The Certificate detail panel gives quick insights into the x509 certificate data by clicking any row.  
Download of Certificate PEM and DER are also supported in the actions menu.
