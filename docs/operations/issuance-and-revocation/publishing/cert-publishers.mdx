## Certificate Publisher

Certificate publishers can be assigned to CAs, Certificate Profiles, or both.  In addition, you can choose to publish on Issuance, Revocation or both events.
Whenever the configured event for a Certificate occurs that matches the selected CA or Certificate Profile, the certificate will be published with the selected configuration.
