## Publishers

Publishers enable automatic upload of PKI artifacts (CRL, Certificates) to external systems when they are generated or on certain events.

Publishers have different types of configurations that enable integration to different external systems using different connections or protocols.

After a configuration is made, a Certificate or CRL publisher can be assigned to your CA or Certificate Profiles. Then
