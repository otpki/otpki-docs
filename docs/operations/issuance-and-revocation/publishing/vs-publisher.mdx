## Validation Service Publisher

Publish CRLs and Certificates to the OTPKI Validation Service. This is the standard location where CRLs would be available in a typical configuration of OTPKI. Because this is an internally supported service no additional configuration is required other than assigning this as a publisher to your CA or Certificate Profiles.
